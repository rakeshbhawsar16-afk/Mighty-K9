Is My Dog Just Getting Older?

"Just Old Age?" — The Signs Worth Paying Attention To

Many of the changes we notice in aging dogs happen gradually — so gradually that it can be easy to brush them off as "normal getting older" behaviour. And while some slowing down is natural, it's worth paying attention to the patterns.

Observable signs like hesitation before stairs, shorter walks, or difficulty getting comfortable may simply reflect age-related changes in joint health and mobility. Understanding what you're seeing is the first step.

Important: If you are concerned about your dog's mobility, comfort, pain levels, or have noticed sudden changes in their movement, please consult your veterinarian. This content is educational and is not a substitute for professional veterinary advice.
  • Taking noticeably longer to stand up from resting
  • Hesitating before heading up or down stairs
  • Wanting to turn back sooner on walks than they used to
  • Less interest in jumping onto furniture or the back seat
  • Spending more time resting during the day
  • Moving with less spring in their step
  • Seeming to take longer to get comfortable before settling
  • Showing less enthusiasm for play or fetch sessions
